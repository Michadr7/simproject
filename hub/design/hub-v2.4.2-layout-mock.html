    Hub 2.4.2: what changes

    You asked: clicking an area must fully open its page; Work is too crowded and squashed to the left. This mock shows the answer. The menu and its effects stay as they are.

    1. The page uses the middle of the window

    The page area runs from the menu to the right edge. Its content sits in the middle and is never wider than 1280 px, so lines stay readable on a very wide window. The two usage gauges move up beside the page title to save height.

    2. The steps leave the menu

    The Work row in the menu keeps only the count of things that need you. The projects and steps are on the Work page.

    Chosen: a list on the left, the open step on the right. The list is sorted by what it needs from you: Needs you, Working, Waiting, Done (folded). One click opens a step beside the list, with one main button. Why: the first thing you see is what to do, and you never lose the list while you read a plan. "Group by: Project" shows the same list as the old tree.

    The one alternative: a board, one column per project (switch at the top). It shows every project at once, but it gets cramped with more than five projects and the open step has to cover part of it.

    3. The panda gets a shelf along the bottom

    A glass shelf, 164 px high, lies under the page area. On the left: what the hub is doing and the last two lines of the live list. The panda walks the whole width when idle and sits at the right when working. The page ends above the shelf, so the shelf never covers or narrows it. The arrow folds it to the small bar of today (the face in the rail brings it back). Why a shelf and not a corner on the right: a wide window has width to spare, and a right corner would narrow the page again at 1400 px.

    Calmer colours, readable text

    Each page keeps its own colour, the flowing background, the gliding highlight and the wave, but all of them are darker and less saturated. The selected menu row is now white, bold text on a deep colour (at least 5.5 to 1; today about 1.3 to 1). The page area lies on a dark glass sheet, so text never sits directly on the moving colours. Worst case anywhere: main text 12 to 1, grey text 5.5 to 1, the faintest grey 4.7 to 1 (it was 3.9 to 1 and is now a lighter grey). The standard asks for 4.5 to 1.

    4. Assets

    The six stage cells run across the top and work as a switch: click a cell to see its rows on the right. Left: new files, the Add asset form, recent changes. Nothing was removed. Why: today all 34 rows are stacked in one long scroll.

    5. Widths

    • 1240 px and wider: menu visible, two columns, panda shelf.
    • 1100 to 1239: menu visible, one column (a step opens in place of the list, with "All work" to go back), panda shelf.
    • Under 1100: the menu folds behind the MENU tab and opens over the page; one or two columns as they fit; the panda is the small bar.
