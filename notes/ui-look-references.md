# UI and look references (owner, 2026-11-18)

Owner likes these better than ours. Source: https://mrdoob.github.io/slop-newyork/ (a three.js game) for the GPS and the speedometer; the third picture is another three.js game (a racing game).

1. SPEEDOMETER (slop-newyork): a dark rounded translucent card; a small grey capital label on top (the vehicle or activity name, "NYC BIKE LANE CYCLIST"), a very large white number, a small grey unit beside it ("MPH"), and a thin rounded progress bar underneath (speed against the top speed). Quiet, lots of padding. Ours: the current speed readout in the driving HUD (find it with Grep in dist/index.html and style.css for speed).
2. GPS / MINIMAP (slop-newyork): a dark rounded-rectangle map, labelled streets in the map itself (grey text on the dark ground, building blocks as slightly lighter shapes), a white arrow for the player with a soft light view cone showing where the camera looks, the route as a thick yellow line, an "N" badge top centre and an "M" (map) badge top right, a small scale/zoom box at the bottom; above it green street-sign chips show the current street and the cross street ("5th Ave", "E 37th St"). The map rotates or stays north-up with the cone showing the view. Ours: the GP1 GPS and the minimap (search PROJECT-INDEX.md for gps / minimap).
3. LOOK (racing game picture): warm low sun with bloom and god rays, a painterly colour grade (posterised clouds, strong orange against blue-violet), lens raindrops, radial motion blur at the screen edges, wet reflective road, a round gear indicator with a speed arc, a round minimap in the bottom left, and a small tip card ("NITRO / HOLD SHIFT FOR A BOOST"). For the look layer (V-A..V-E): grade, sun bloom/rays, edge motion blur at speed, optional rain drops on the lens while it rains.

Plan: a HUD restyle stage (speedometer card + GPS card with street-sign chips and the view cone) goes with the look layer; Sonnet can build it as CSS/canvas without touching the shared files if the HUD markup is in index.html and style.css (game.js hooks by exact edit list).
