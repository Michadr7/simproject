## Findings
1. Seat: anchor_driver_seat [0,.86,-.3] (matches CLASSES.atv seat z -.30). Hip joint = anchor y minus .03. Pose is the existing character.js seated pose (thighs forward, shins down) via avatar.setSeated(true) and seatPose (body tilts with the vehicle pitch/roll/yaw).
2. Hands: anchor_hands_L/R [+-.37,1.05,.23] via avatar.reach() two-bone IK; feet are NOT IK-placed (the seated pose has no foot IK); the shins just hang. By arithmetic the feet land near z +.15, y .38 against the footrest anchors (+-.36,.35,0): close but unverified, and the knees are together (no splayed astride legs).
3. Handlebar: createProp exposes userData.nodes.handlebar [0,.7,.34] but the geometry is merged into the body, so it cannot turn. NOT faked. Instead the hand targets are rotated about the handlebar node by steer*0.5 rad, so the hands/arms swing with the steer while the bar model stays still. The model would need a separate mesh node named handlebar (or steer_bar) pivoting at [0,.7,.34] (a sixth draw call) to turn the visible bar; then riderHands can drive that pivot too. The torso is not turned (only hands), by design, to stay minimal.
4. Front wheels: record has 4 wheels, 2 with front:true and pivots; steer cap applied through steerMax .52.
5. Camera: CLASSES.atv.camera {back:4.2,up:1.9} is used via driving.cameraZoom()/cameraPitch() (game.js lines 760, 885, 1483). Verified by reading, not seen.
6. Jiggle: y offset, amplitude .012 m at top speed, two summed sines (23 and 37.7 rad/s) of the driving clock `pulse` (advanced by dt, so frame-rate independent). Speed-based only; no surface roughness input (kept cheap).
7. Passenger: cls.seats===1; seats has only a driver entry so seatedAt for passenger is null. The door logic for seats:1 was already A4.
8. Frame rate: nothing heavy. Two extra IK reaches per frame while on the ATV (same cost as the shortbed's two), a few trig calls. No new draw calls, materials or allocations (riderHand makes 1 small object per hand per frame; could be pooled if it ever shows in a profile).
