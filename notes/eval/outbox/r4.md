## How the patches work
At every graph node touched by a wild road, legs are sorted by angle and each open wedge of 20..150 degrees gets a fillet (corner where the two road edges cross, then a tangent arc, radius 6 asphalt/asphalt, 4 asphalt apron when a gravel leg is involved, tangent length capped at 14 m). Square asphalt Ts (haul road, Bay Road, Quay/Ferry streets) stay with the old pass 1 (no double patch). Result: 9 new patches at 4 joins: (600,68) Blackstone access x spine (2 aprons), (600,-200) car link x access road (2), (520,330) Coast Link + P5 spur off Ferry Street (3 wedges incl. the 24 degree Coast Link wedge), (640,68) Coast Link Y on the spine (2: 65 and 115 degrees). 28 patches in total (was 19). (290,68) is two collinear 12 m roads: nothing to patch.

## Kaspian Bay in TOWNS
There is no 'kaspian' zone (the bay is outside the old ground, zones must lie inside it), so TOWNS holds the id for names/signs and does NOT suppress patches there (the bay's T joins need them). If a zone is added later, patches inside it would be suppressed like the other towns: decide then.
