## New Pose Output Fields
`workforcePose(out, person, day, minute, ctx)` now writes four additional fields on `out`:
- `trunkX` (`number`, `NaN` when no felled trunk is on the ground; `currTree.tx` during `buck` while `progress < BUCK_CUT_END`)
- `trunkZ` (`number`, `NaN` when no felled trunk is on the ground; `currTree.tz` during `buck` while `progress < BUCK_CUT_END`)
- `trunkYaw` (`number`, `NaN` when no felled trunk is on the ground; `out.yaw + Math.PI / 2` during `buck` while `progress < BUCK_CUT_END`)
- `groundLogs` (`number`, `0` normally; `2` during the post-cut pause `buck` `progress >= BUCK_CUT_END` and `bendPick1`; `1` during `carry1`, `bendDrop1`, `walkBack1`, `bendPick2`; `0` from `carry2` onward)
