## Method
- `blocked` = game.js worldBlocked rebuilt in Node: `!isWalkable || createWorld().blocked || MAP.buildings boxes (+0.33 m margin; shops with the doorway gap)`. `cost` = the game's surface cost (pavement/path/driveway 1, road/street 2.5, else 1.4).
- Every block of every one of the 28 people on days 1-7 (schedule.createSchedule, no roster wrapper): 483 travel blocks, 87 drive (hidden, no route), 396 on foot = 45 unique place pairs. Each pair routed once with `findRoute` (as the crowd does, cell 2 m, bounds MAP.playable).
- Route checked by sampling every 0.5 m (ends within DOOR_CLEAR ignored). Needed time = length / WALK_SPEED (2.1 m/s) against block minutes x 2 s.

## What this means for the design
- A* is good enough on the real map: no failures, no wall hits worth a path net (N6 is not urgent).
- Caching: the crowd already caches by (from,to) and runs 1 search/frame, so the 45 pairs fill over 45 frames at worst; a 21 ms search in one frame is a visible hitch (a 165 FPS frame is 6 ms). Options: (a) precompute all 45 pairs at load (about 0.4 s, behind the loading screen) and drop ROUTES_PER_FRAME to 0 for known pairs; (b) keep lazy, but only run a search when the person is within the draw radius (already so). (a) is the cheap safe choice; N2 adds more pairs (visits, loops), so make the cache a shared module keyed by pair.
- Fix schedule.js DETOUR (1.3 -> about 1.5 for road-crossing pairs) or compute block length from real route lengths (the 'A2 replaces the estimate' comment in population.js): affects forbidden-for-me files? schedule.js and population.js are NOT on the forbidden list, but changing them changes every plan (tests/population.mjs, schedule tests); not done here.
